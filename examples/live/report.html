What the grader checks

Actual disposition, ticket state, refund amount and count, refund target, and order state. Expected answers are held outside role context. Policy failures remain visible because the simulated action tool does not repair them.

The suite is authored alongside this prototype. Its holdout varies values and wording within the same scenario families. Small samples and related cases limit generalization.
